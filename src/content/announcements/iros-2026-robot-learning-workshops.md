The IEEE RAS Technical Committee on Robot Learning was pleased to co-organize two workshops and support three additional workshops at IROS 2026 in Pittsburgh, USA. Together, these workshops brought the community together around foundation models, field and space robotics, industrial applications, and data-driven control.

## Co-organized workshops

- **Foundation Models in Multi-Robot Systems** — Sunday, September 27, 8:30 a.m.-12:30 p.m., Rooms 301 & 302
- **Data in Field Robotics: From State Estimation to Navigation** — Thursday, October 1, 1:30-5:30 p.m., Room 317/318

## Supported workshops

- **Space Robotics Workshop: Space Exploration and Sustained Operations Beyond Earth** — Sunday, September 27, 8:30 a.m.-12:30 p.m., Room 335
- **1st International Workshop on Industrial Applications of Robot Learning** — Sunday, September 27, 8:30 a.m.-5:30 p.m., Room 407
- **Sim2Real and Classical Control: From Rigorous Theory to Data-Driven Robotics** — Thursday, October 1, 8:30 a.m.-5:30 p.m., Rooms 303 & 304

Thank you to the organizers, speakers, and attendees who contributed to these IROS 2026 workshops and the exchange of ideas across the robot learning community.
